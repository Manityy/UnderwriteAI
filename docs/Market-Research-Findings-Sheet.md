# UnderwriteAI — Market Research Findings
**Meeting sheet · Team Pivot · July 2026**

**Product in one line:** An X-ray for AI risk — we help insurers score and condition coverage on clients’ AI systems, without needing the source code. We do **not** sell insurance.

---

## 1. The problem

| | |
|---|---|
| **Pain** | Companies deploy AI everywhere. Insurers want to cover what can go wrong, but AI is a black box — no source code, weak questionnaires. |
| **Result today** | Decline, overprice, or accept with weak conditions. |
| **Core line** | *You can’t price what you can’t see.* |

---

## 2. Who buys (ICP)

**Primary buyers:** Insurance companies, reinsurers, and brokers — **not** the company that built the AI.

### Tunisia (start here)
| Priority | Names |
|---|---|
| **#1** | **Tunis Re** + broker **IRIS** (cyber product launched Jan 2026) |
| **Next** | STAR, COMAR, Maghrebia, GAT, ASTREE, BIAT, Lloyd, BH, BNA, Carte |
| **Later** | AMI, CTAMA, MAE, takaful multi-branch (Zitouna, At-takafulia, El Amana) |
| **Weak fit** | Life-only insurers |

### Abroad (scale later)
Europe specialty/cyber carriers & MGAs → MENA Gulf → large reinsurers (e.g. Munich Re, Swiss Re) as later accounts.

---

## 3. Is it needed?

| Market | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **Tunisia** | **Early but real.** Cyber insurance is just starting. AI use is growing (banks, fintech, startups, tech) — not yet everywhere (most traditional SMEs). Best as a **pilot market**, not a huge revenue market alone. |
| **Abroad** | **Stronger need.** Global cyber ~$15–20B; AI liability emerging; EU AI Act pressure. |

**Tunisian AI reality:** Banks (chatbots, fraud, scoring), startups/tech, digital clients = relevant. Average small shop = not yet.

---

**How we make money:** SaaS / per assessment — **not** a share of the full insurance premium pool.

---

## 5. Competitors

| Type | Who | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct** (same product for insurers) | — | **None found** (esp. Tunisia) |
| Local “competition” | Excel + questionnaires + gut feel | What we replace |
| AI security / red-team | Promptfoo, Garak, Lakera… | Sell to tech/security teams, not underwriters |
| AI governance | Credo AI, Holistic AI… | Compliance ≠ insurability score |
| UW software | Federato, Cytora, Sixfold… | Faster submissions, not AI behavior X-ray |
| Cyber policy sellers | Coalition, At-Bay, Tunis Re Cyber… | **Policies** — buyers/partners, not software rivals |
| Big reinsurers | Munich Re, Swiss Re | **Potential buyers** (later); may build tools too |

**Wedge:** Buyer = insurer · No source code · Output = score + conditions + accept/decline · Tunisia first-mover angle.

---

## 6. Will it work?

| Why yes | Why hard |
|---|---|
| Real pain + clear wedge | Tunisia alone is too small |
| Cyber launching locally; AI rising | Insurers buy slowly |
| Almost no direct tool competitor | Clients may refuse live AI tests |
| Demo already shows ask + test + score | Must sell practical UW value, not “$22B AI insurance” hype |

**Verdict:** Works as **Tunisia pilot → Europe/MENA scale**. Needs 1–2 real insurer/broker pilots to prove it.

---

## 7. Buying criteria & objections (expect these)

**They will care about:** explainability, speed, French UX, PDF/report for committee, low pilot price, “insurer still decides.”

**Common objections:** “We already have questionnaires” · “Clients won’t let you probe” · “Too early for Tunisia” · “Legal/data risk” · “Can we trust an LLM judge?”

---

## 8. What to build / do next

1. Live endpoint testing (biggest credibility jump)  
2. Underwriting export pack (PDF + conditions + rationale)  
3. Consent / safe-test / questionnaire-only fallback  
4. French-first wording  
5. Broker → insurer workflow (IRIS-style)  
6. **Validate:** 8–12 Tunisia interviews + pilot with Tunis Re / IRIS path  

---

## 9. One-slide takeaway for the room

1. **Problem:** Insurers can’t see inside AI → can’t price it.  
2. **Buyer:** Insurers / reinsurers / brokers (Tunisia first: Tunis Re, IRIS, STAR…).  
3. **Need:** Early in Tunisia, stronger abroad; AI used mainly by banks & tech.  
4. **Competitors:** No direct tool twin; nearby = security, governance, UW software.  
5. **Strategy:** Pilot in Tunisia → scale abroad. Prove value, then grow.
